On the record

How I think about marketing — and where I apply it.

  • Marketing starts with the business

    A marketing strategy should be built around the business model, commercial goal and buyer. The right mix for a SaaS company is not automatically the right mix for a legal-services business.

  • Buyer behaviour matters more than channel fashion

    SEO, LinkedIn, paid media, email, events and referrals are tools. The strategy starts with where the customer actually researches, evaluates and buys.

  • Legal services are sold on trust

    When the product is expertise, skilled labour or technology-enabled delivery, positioning has to make that expertise visible before the buyer experiences the service.

  • AI should improve the commercial system

    I use Generative AI and automation pragmatically — to accelerate research, qualification, content workflows and campaign execution without replacing judgement.

  • Current domain focus

    Legal services, ALSPs, LPO, eDiscovery, managed document review, LegalTech, legal operations and Contract Lifecycle Management.
